VOOHU WHSG24301TG: A Drop-In Replacement for the iNRCORE GBE-5032NL LAN Transformer

If you are looking for a pin-compatible, cost-effective alternative to the iNRCORE (formerly Pulse Electronics) GBE-5032NL 1000BASE-T LAN transformer, the VOOHU WHSG24301TG is a true pin-for-pin drop-in. This guide cross-references both parts so you can substitute with confidence.

Originally published on the VOOHU official site: voohuele.com/news/2026-06-17-2

1. Competitor overview — iNRCORE GBE-5032NL

iNRCORE, LLC (USA) is the former Pulse Electronics networking-magnetics business. The GBE-5032NL is a 1000BASE-T single-port, 4-channel-integrated, industrial-grade low-profile SMD transformer module in a 24-pin package, widely used in Gigabit Ethernet hardware.

2. VOOHU replacement — WHSG24301TG

The VOOHU WHSG24301TG (Suzhou Voohu Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.) is a 100/1000BASE-T single-port (4-channel integrated) low-profile SMD transformer module in a fully pin-compatible 24-pin package — pinout identical to the GBE-5032NL. It delivers equivalent functionality with stronger cost-effectiveness and supply stability.

4. Compatibility notes

  • Both operate over -40°C to +85°C (industrial wide temperature) and share an identical pinout, footprint and circuit topology (1CT:1CT × 4 channels) in a low-profile 24-pin package — enabling true pin-for-pin replacement.
  • Both rate 1500 V isolation, but with different test durations (GBE-5032NL 5 s min; WHSG24301TG 60 s).
  • Storage temperature: the GBE-5032NL range (-55°C ~ +125°C) is wider than the WHSG24301TG (-40°C ~ +85°C); operating temperatures are identical.
  • Package dimensions are close (1.27 mm pin pitch, 13.97 mm pin span, pad-compatible), both low-profile; the VOOHU height of 2.85 mm is slightly taller than the GBE's 2.24 mm — verify enclosure clearance.
  • The GBE-5032NL datasheet does not list leakage inductance or DCR; the WHSG24301TG datasheet does not state RoHS / MSL / certification — confirm with VOOHU.

6. Frequently asked questions

Q1: Is the WHSG24301TG fully pin-compatible with the GBE-5032NL — can it replace it without a PCB change?
Yes. Both use a 24-pin low-profile SMD package with 1.27 mm pin pitch and 13.97 mm pin span, an identical 4-channel pinout (matching TD±/TDCT and TX±/TXCT positions) and the same per-channel 1CT:1CT topology. The WHSG24301TG is a direct drop-in with no PCB redesign; only verify enclosure height clearance (GBE 2.24 mm vs VOOHU 2.85 mm).
Q2: Do both parts share the same temperature grade?
The operating temperature is identical — both -40°C to +85°C (industrial wide temperature), suitable for industrial Ethernet, data centers and temperature-variable environments. The GBE storage range (-55°C ~ +125°C) is slightly wider, but this does not affect normal use or substitution.
