SFP Cage Assembly Review: A Markdown Checklist for Board and Panel Fit

An SFP port needs a review of the physical assembly as well as the electrical design. For teams evaluating VOOHU cages and connectors, the most useful checklist identifies the exact cage, host connector, board, panel, and intended module before recording any fit result.

VOOHU's documented product range includes SFP, SFP+, SFP28, and QSFP+ cage families, electrical connectors, and cage variants with heat sinks or light pipes. These are useful selection categories. Each proposed assembly still requires the drawing and interface information for its particular components.

Define exactly which components are being reviewed

A metal cage is a mechanical and shielding component, not an optical transceiver. Keep the cage, electrical connector, and module as separate entries in the bill of materials, even when a supplier offers items together. State whether the enquiry covers the cage alone or also includes the electrical connector and accessories.

Record the intended form-factor family and port arrangement before comparing drawings. A family name alone does not establish compatibility across SFP, SFP+, SFP28, and QSFP configurations, nor does a cage selection establish the equipment's data rate. Keep those questions linked to the exact host and module requirements.

Review the assembly in its installed position

Compare the cage and connector drawings with the board footprint, mounting features, panel cutout, and board-to-panel location. Record how the shield contacts are intended to meet the chassis. Include the selected assembly method and any component-specific installation requirements in the review package.

For a light-pipe variant, check the documented light-pipe position against the panel indicators and board LEDs. For a heat-sink variant, review its geometry, surrounding space, and planned airflow. The presence of a heat sink is a configuration detail; it does not supply a verified thermal result for the finished equipment.

Use “pass,” “open,” or “not applicable” for each item. A pass should cite evidence from the actual proposed configuration, with the reviewer and date recorded below the table.

Close the review with a specific product request

Check module-latch and removal access with adjacent ports and the enclosure included. Keep an unperformed check open, and attach revised evidence when the board or panel changes. This prevents an early drawing review from being mistaken for acceptance of the finished assembly.

VOOHU's materials describe technical documentation and design-file support. Ask which drawings and 3D files are available for the cage and connector being considered.
