RJ45 Connector FAQs: Answering the Top 15 Questions from Engineers

After serving 1,500+ customers globally, our engineering team at VOOHU Electronics has fielded thousands of technical questions about RJ45 connectors. This article compiles the 15 most frequently asked questions — with clear, actionable answers you can use immediately in your design or procurement process.

1. Should I choose an integrated or discrete RJ45 connector?

Integrated RJ45 (with built-in magnetics) saves ~30% PCB space, guarantees EMI compliance, and cuts development to ~1 week. Discrete RJ45 (separate transformer) offers full magnetics flexibility and can be cheaper at 1M+ volumes. For most standard Ethernet designs, integrated wins. See our full comparison guide →

2. What is the difference between Cat5e, Cat6, and Cat6A RJ45 connectors?

The bandwidth: Cat5e = 100 MHz, Cat6 = 250 MHz, Cat6A = 500 MHz. Higher categories require tighter internal tolerances to minimize crosstalk and return loss. Using a Cat5e connector on Cat6 cable bottlenecks your link to 100 MHz.

3. Can any RJ45 connector be used for PoE?

No. PoE demands gold-plated contacts (6µ" minimum), ≤ 20 mΩ contact resistance, and adequate dielectric strength (1500 V AC for 4PPoE). Standard connectors may arc or overheat. VOOHU's PoE-optimized RJ45 series supports 802.3af/at/bt up to 90W.

4. What IP rating do I need for outdoor RJ45 connectors?

IP65 = water jets, IP67 = temporary immersion (1m water, 30 min). Outdoor cabinets in rain need IP65 minimum. Flood-prone or washdown areas need IP67. The enclosure must match the connector's rating. Explore VOOHU's waterproof IP67 RJ45 →

5. How do I test an RJ45 connector before production?

Five essential tests: (1) Insertion loss / return loss with a VNA, (2) HiPot at 1500 V AC for PoE, (3) Contact resistance via four-wire Kelvin method, (4) Full mating cycle durability test, (5) Environmental stress (thermal shock, humidity). See our validation guide →

6. What are the advantages of XLR locking RJ45 over standard RJ45?

The plastic snap tab is replaced by a metal self-locking mechanism that won't vibrate loose. 750+ mating cycles. Compatible with Neutrik etherCON. Ideal for stage lighting, industrial machinery, and railway. See VOOHU's XLR locking RJ45 →

7. What is Single Pair Ethernet (SPE)?

SPE transmits data + power over one twisted pair, reaching 1000m with 10Base-T1L. Uses compact 2-pin IEC 63171-6 connectors. Perfect for IIoT sensors and actuators. See VOOHU's SPE connectors →

8. How do I avoid RJ45 connector failures in PoE applications?

Top failures: contact arcing, overheating, surge damage. Prevention: gold-plated contacts, layered protection (GDT + TVS + ESD), and ≤ 20 mΩ contact resistance. Full guide: PoE failure prevention →

12. Do I need a shielded or unshielded RJ45 connector?

Shielded (metal housing) for F/UTP or S/FTP cables in high-EMI environments. Unshielded (plastic) is fine for UTP in offices. When in doubt, use shielded — it also provides ground continuity.

13. What is the difference between solid and stranded core RJ45 connectors?

Solid core connectors use IDC for rigid conductors. Stranded core uses piercing contacts for flexible wires. Wrong type = unreliable termination. Always match the connector to the cable type.
