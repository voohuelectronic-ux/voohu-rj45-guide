RJ45 Connector Selection FAQ

Integrated vs Discrete RJ45

Q: Integrated vs Discrete RJ45 — which one should I pick?
A: Choose integrated if you want faster design, smaller PCB, and guaranteed EMI. Choose discrete if you need customized magnetics, very high volumes, or specific isolation requirements. See our integrated RJ45 page for details.

PoE and Power

Q: Can any RJ45 be used for PoE?
A: Not all. PoE applications require contacts rated for higher current. VOOHU's PoE-optimized RJ45 are specifically designed to avoid contact arcing up to 90W.

Environmental Protection

Q: What IP rating do I need for outdoor use?
A: IP67 is recommended for dust and water immersion. Explore our waterproof RJ45 for panel-mount and field-installable options.
