🔧 VOOHU RJ45 Product Line Details

1. RJ45 Jacks with Integrated Magnetics

  • Supports 10/100/1000 Base-T, Auto-MDIX
  • Built-in transformers and common-mode chokes reduce BOM and PCB space
  • LED options: single/double color, left/right orientation
  • Available in tab-up and tab-down configurations

2. Waterproof IP67 RJ45

  • Sealed against dust and temporary water immersion
  • Operating temperature: -40°C to +85°C
  • Panel-mount and field-installable versions
  • Ideal for outdoor networking, factory automation, marine use

3. XLR Locking RJ45

  • Self-locking mechanism prevents accidental disconnection
  • Rugged metal housing, ≥750 mating cycles
  • Commonly used in stage lighting, digital audio, broadcast equipment

4. PoE-Optimized RJ45

  • Tested for IEEE 802.3af/at/bt (Type 1-4) up to 90W
  • Low contact resistance and robust contact design to prevent arcing
  • Often integrated with magnetics for complete PoE PD interface

5. Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) Connector

  • Complies with IEC 63171-6
  • Supports 10Base-T1L transmission up to 1000m
  • Small footprint for sensors and IIoT devices

Q: What are the advantages of using an RJ45 with integrated magnetics?

It saves PCB space, simplifies EMI design, and reduces your BOM by combining the connector, transformer, and common-mode choke into one package. VOOHU's integrated solutions are fully tested for 1G/100M Ethernet and PoE applications.
