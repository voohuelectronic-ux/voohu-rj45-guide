XLR Locking RJ45 Connectors

VOOHU's XLR locking RJ45 connectors (also known as XLR-style RJ45 or etherCON-compatible RJ45) merge the mechanical robustness of professional XLR locking mechanisms with standard RJ45 Ethernet data transmission. By replacing the fragile plastic snap tab with a rugged self-locking metal structure, these connectors eliminate accidental disconnections caused by vibration, cable tugging, or repeated handling — making them the trusted choice for professional audio, stage lighting, broadcast, and industrial automation.
