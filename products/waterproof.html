Waterproof IP67 RJ45 Connectors

VOOHU's waterproof RJ45 connectors are purpose-built for outdoor and harsh-environment Ethernet connectivity. With IP65 to IP67 ingress protection ratings, these connectors withstand dust, heavy rain, temporary water immersion, and corrosive atmospheres. Whether mounted on an outdoor enclosure panel or field-installed on a cable in a factory floor, VOOHU's waterproof RJ45 series ensures your network stays up — no matter the weather.

IP Rating Explained: What Level Do You Need?

RatingDust ProtectionWater ProtectionBest For
IP65Dust-tightWater jets from any directionOutdoor cabinets with rain exposure
IP67Dust-tightImmersion in 1m water for 30 minFlood-prone areas, marine, underground vaults

Important: A waterproof RJ45 with IP67 rating does not guarantee the entire device is IP67. Panel sealing gaskets, enclosure seams, and all other connector ports must match the same protection level for full system certification.
