Integrated RJ45 Jacks with Magnetics

VOOHU's integrated RJ45 solutions embed network transformers and common-mode chokes directly into the connector housing. This integration significantly reduces component count, saves up to 30% PCB area, and guarantees EMI compliance for 10/100/1000 Base-T applications.

Key Benefits

  • ✔ Reduced BOM – eliminates separate transformer module
  • ✔ Faster Time-to-Market – pre-validated EMI design cuts development from 3 weeks to 1 week
  • ✔ Flexible Configurations – 1x1, 2x1, tab-up/down, with/without LED
  • ✔ Full Speed Support – 100M and 1000M (Gigabit) options

Typical Applications

  • Industrial switches, IP cameras, embedded systems, factory automation, building controllers.
