Integrated RJ45 Jacks with Magnetics

VOOHU's integrated RJ45 connectors embed the network transformer and common-mode choke directly into the connector housing. This all-in-one design reduces component count, saves up to 30% PCB area, and guarantees EMI compliance for 10/100/1000 Base-T applications — making it the go-to choice for engineers who need reliability without the design headache.
