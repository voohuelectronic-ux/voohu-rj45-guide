Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) Connectors

SPE is the future of industrial connectivity. VOOHU's SPE connectors conform to IEC 63171-6 and support 10Base-T1L, delivering data and power over a single twisted pair up to 1000 meters. Ideal for smart sensors, actuators, and process automation.

Advantages

  • ✔ Small footprint for compact devices
  • ✔ Up to 1000m reach
  • ✔ Reduces cabling weight and cost
  • ✔ Compatible with advanced PHY chips
