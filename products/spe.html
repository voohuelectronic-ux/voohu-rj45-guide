Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) Connectors

Single Pair Ethernet (SPE) is the future of industrial field-level connectivity. Unlike traditional 4-pair Ethernet, SPE transmits data and power over just one twisted pair of wires — dramatically reducing cable weight, cost, and installation complexity. VOOHU's SPE connectors comply with IEC 63171-6 and support 10Base-T1L communication up to 1000 meters, making them the ideal physical interface for smart sensors, actuators, and IIoT devices in Industry 4.0 architectures.

Why SPE? The Case for Single Pair

Traditional 4-Pair EthernetVOOHU Single Pair Ethernet (SPE)
4 pairs (8 wires) — bulky, heavy cable1 pair (2 wires) — up to 70% lighter, smaller bend radius
Limited to ~100m without repeatersUp to 1000m reach with 10Base-T1L
Standard RJ45 (8P8C) connectorCompact 2-pin IEC 63171-6 connector
Requires PoE injector/switch for powerIntegrated PoDL (Power over Data Line) up to 50W
Designed for office/enterprise LANPurpose-built for industrial field level, replacing fieldbuses
