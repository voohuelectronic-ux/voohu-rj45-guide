PoE-Optimized RJ45 Connectors

Power over Ethernet demands robust connectors. VOOHU's PoE RJ45 series is designed to handle up to 90W (IEEE 802.3bt Type 4) with enhanced contact design that prevents arcing and ensures reliable power delivery.

Key Features

  • ✔ Compliant with 802.3af/at/bt
  • ✔ High-current contacts, low contact resistance
  • ✔ Compatible with VOOHU PoE transformers and protection devices (TVS/ESD)

How PoE Works

DC power is injected into the center tap of the Ethernet transformer and transmitted over the twisted pairs along with data. VOOHU offers complete reference designs for PoE PD interfaces.
