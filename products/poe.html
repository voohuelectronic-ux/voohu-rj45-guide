PoE-Optimized RJ45 Connectors

Power over Ethernet (PoE) demands more from an RJ45 connector. When DC power flows through the same contacts that carry high-speed data, standard RJ45 jacks can suffer from contact arcing, increased resistance, and premature failure. VOOHU's PoE-optimized RJ45 connectors are engineered with enhanced contact design, thicker gold plating, and robust current-carrying capacity to handle the full spectrum of PoE standards — from basic 802.3af (15W) to high-power 802.3bt Type 4 (90W).

How PoE Works Through an RJ45

PoE delivers DC power over the same twisted pairs used for Ethernet data. The key principle:

  • 100BASE-T (Fast Ethernet): Data uses pairs 2 and 3. Power can be injected on the unused spare pairs (1 and 4) or superimposed on data pairs via the transformer center tap.
  • 1000BASE-T (Gigabit): All 4 pairs carry data. Power must be injected via the transformer center tap — known as "phantom power."
  • Isolation: The network transformer provides 1500Vrms isolation between the RJ45 cable side and the PHY chip side, protecting sensitive electronics from cable-borne surges.

For a complete PoE PD (Powered Device) interface, VOOHU recommends: PoE RJ45 → PoE Power Transformer → Bridge Rectifier → PD Controller → DC-DC Converter, with TVS diodes at the RJ45 front-end for ESD and surge protection.
